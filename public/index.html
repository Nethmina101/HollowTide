How a voyage works

Each player receives a secret role after the captain starts. Crew: move to the glowing square for each repair and press E; complete all six repairs or vote out every Hollow. The Hollow: use Q to snuff lights, use the sabotage buttons to create critical threats, and click a nearby Crew player to eliminate them. Press Space near a body to report it, or use it away from a body to call a meeting (one-minute cooldown). During a meeting, vote or skip.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys to move · E interact with a nearby repair · Space report / ring bell · Q snuff lights.
